Privacy policy - minicrm VPS backup

Last updated: November 2026

minicrm VPS backup is operated by Zvi Naiman (ibexprogrammer@gmail.com). It is described on its home page. This policy covers the Google data used by this Google project: the backup tool, and Google Sign-In on a private test copy of the Jewish Points CRM.

Backup tool

The tool connects to one Google account, the operator's own, and requests the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. That permission lets the tool see, edit, create and delete only the specific Drive files that the user uses with this app. The tool does not request your name, email, contacts, Gmail or other Google data.

The files uploaded are encrypted copies of the Jewish Points CRM database. They are encrypted before they are uploaded, stored in the operator's Google Drive, and not shared with anyone. The operator does not sell or share this data, and uses it only to restore the CRM after a failure.

Retention policy: daily copies are kept for 14 days. Older copies are deleted.

The operator can remove the tool's access at any time in the Google Account's third-party access settings.

Google Sign-In on the test copy

The test copy of the CRM lets approved users sign in with Google. It uses the ID token that Google returns. The CRM reads your email address from it to match you to an existing CRM account, and stores your Google account ID the first time you sign in. If you register a new organization with Google, it also reads and stores your name. This sign-in does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail or contacts.

This data is used to log you in and run your CRM account. It is not sold or shared for advertising. To ask for your sign-in data to be removed, write to the contact address below.
